# One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

One sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — in words they
already know, one picture they can hold in their head.

```
Before your candle burns an inch, you can send a letter to the far side of the world and hold the answer in your hand before the wax has cooled.
```

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
